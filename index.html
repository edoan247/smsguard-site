GOOGLE DRIVE BACKUP

Your backups stay inside SMSGuard's private app data area.

When you choose Google Drive backup, SMSGuard requests access only to the Google Drive appDataFolder scope used by the app.

  • SMSGuard does not browse your personal Google Drive files.
  • SMSGuard does not read documents, photos, or folders outside its private app storage.
  • Google Drive access is used only for SMSGuard backup and restore.
☁

Limited Drive permission

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata

This scope allows SMSGuard to store and retrieve its own hidden app backup files.
