Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

SMSGuard respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app may access, use, store, process, and, when you choose to use Google Drive backup, transfer to your personal Google Drive account.

SMSGuard is a messaging and spam protection application. The app uses certain permissions and information only to provide messaging, filtering, blocking, contact display, dual SIM, backup and restore, notification, premium, and security features.

Important: SMSGuard uses SMS/MMS message content, sender and recipient phone numbers, and contact information only for the app's messaging, protection, backup, and restore features. SMSGuard does not use this information for advertising or marketing purposes.

1. SMS and MMS Messages

SMSGuard may access SMS and MMS messages on your device to provide its core messaging features.

This may include:

  • Sending SMS messages
  • Receiving SMS messages
  • Receiving MMS messages
  • Displaying SMS/MMS conversations
  • Managing message history
  • Blocking unwanted messages
  • Filtering spam messages
  • Applying keyword filters
  • Supporting scheduled messages and message-related app features
  • Creating and restoring user-requested backups

SMS and MMS message content is normally processed locally on your device to provide messaging and protection features.

Spam detection, number filtering, keyword filtering, and message management are processed locally on your device.

SMSGuard does not upload SMS/MMS message content to SMSGuard-owned servers or Firebase backend services for community spam reporting, spam analysis, advertising, or marketing.

If you explicitly choose to create a Google Drive backup, selected SMS/MMS-related backup data may be uploaded to the private app-specific data area of your own Google Drive account as described in the Google Drive Backup and Restore section below.

2. Phone Numbers

SMSGuard may access and use sender and recipient phone numbers found in SMS/MMS conversations on your device.

This includes phone numbers found in regular, blocked, spam, sent, and received conversations.

Phone number information may be used for:

  • Sending and receiving SMS messages
  • Showing conversation lists
  • Displaying senders and recipients
  • Recognizing phone numbers
  • Blocking selected phone numbers
  • Applying number-based filters
  • Applying spam protection rules
  • Supporting dual SIM messaging
  • Matching phone numbers with saved contacts when contact permission is granted
  • Displaying saved contact names in message lists, conversations, and notifications
  • Creating and restoring user-requested backups

SMSGuard does not upload sender or recipient phone numbers to SMSGuard-owned servers or Firebase backend services for community spam reporting, spam analysis, advertising, or marketing.

If you choose Google Drive backup, phone numbers contained in the selected backup data may be included in the backup stored in your personal Google Drive app-specific data area.

3. Contacts

If you grant contact permission, SMSGuard may access contacts stored on your device.

Contacts are used to match phone numbers with saved contact names and display those names in conversations, message lists, recipient selection screens, and notifications.

SMSGuard does not upload your contact list to SMSGuard-owned servers or Firebase backend services for spam analysis, advertising, or marketing.

Contact information is used only for contact matching and messaging-related features.

SMSGuard does not automatically upload your complete device contact list as part of Google Drive backup.

4. Dual SIM and Phone State Information

On supported devices, SMSGuard may access phone state or SIM subscription information to provide dual SIM messaging features.

This may be used to:

  • Show which SIM card is used for messages
  • Show which SIM received a message
  • Allow SIM selection when sending messages
  • Improve messaging behavior on dual SIM devices

This information is used only for messaging-related features.

5. Spam Protection

SMSGuard provides spam protection using features such as:

  • Number-based filters
  • Keyword filters
  • Unknown sender blocking
  • Manual spam-folder actions
  • Local spam detection

These spam protection features are processed locally on the user's device.

SMSGuard does not operate a community spam-reporting system and does not upload sender phone numbers or SMS/MMS message content to SMSGuard-owned servers or Firebase backend services for community spam reporting or spam analysis.

Blocked messages and filtering rules may be included in a Google Drive backup only when the user chooses to create such a backup.

6. Google Drive Backup and Restore

SMSGuard provides an optional Google Drive backup and restore feature.

Google Drive backup and restore are initiated by the user and require authorization through the user's Google account.

SMSGuard requests access to the following Google Drive scope:

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata

This permission allows SMSGuard to store and retrieve its own backup files in the private app-specific data area of the user's Google Drive account.

SMSGuard does not request general access to browse or manage the user's other Google Drive files.

SMSGuard cannot use this permission to browse files outside its own app-specific data area.

Depending on the backup options and app version, a backup may contain information such as:

  • SMS message data
  • MMS-related message data and supported attachments
  • Sender and recipient phone numbers associated with backed-up messages
  • Blocked messages
  • Blocked-number rules
  • Keyword filtering rules
  • Selected application settings and preferences
  • Other SMSGuard data specifically included in the backup feature

Google Drive backups are stored in the user's own Google Drive app-specific data area and are not stored on SMSGuard-owned servers.

SMSGuard does not use Google Drive backup data for advertising, marketing, profiling, community spam reporting, or unrelated analytics.

Google processes Google account authorization and Google Drive services according to Google's own privacy policies and terms.

Uninstalling SMSGuard or clearing the app's local data does not necessarily delete an existing Google Drive backup. Backup data stored in Google Drive may remain in the user's Google account until it is removed using available Google Drive or Google account controls, or an applicable deletion feature provided by the app.

Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Google Drive backups are separate from local application data. Clearing application data or uninstalling the application may not delete backups already stored in your Google Drive app-specific data area.

Users may use available Google Drive or Google account controls to manage or remove app-specific Google Drive data.

14. Data Retention

Locally stored data remains on the device until it is deleted by the user, removed by an application feature, cleared through Android settings, or removed when the application is uninstalled, subject to Android system behavior.

Google Drive backup data may remain in the user's Google Drive app-specific data area until it is deleted through applicable Google Drive, Google account, or SMSGuard controls.
