Google Drive backup

SMSGuard may request the Google Drive app-specific data scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata) when the user chooses Google Drive backup or restore. This permission is used only to store and retrieve SMSGuard backup files in the app's private Google Drive app data area.

SMSGuard does not use this permission to browse, read, modify, or list the user's personal Google Drive files outside the app-specific data area.
